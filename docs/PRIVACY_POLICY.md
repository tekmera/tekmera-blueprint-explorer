# Tekmera Fusion Explorer - Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025

## 1. Introduction

Tekmera Inc. ("Tekmera," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use Tekmera Fusion Explorer ("the Software").

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide
- **License Information**: Email address and name provided during license purchase
- **Support Communications**: Information provided when contacting customer support
- **Payment Information**: Processed by third-party payment processors (Stripe, etc.)

### 2.2 Information Automatically Collected
- **No Telemetry**: The Software does NOT automatically collect usage data, analytics, or telemetry
- **No Tracking**: We do not track your usage patterns or behavior within the Software
- **Local Operation**: The Software operates entirely on your local machine

### 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect
- Blueprint file contents or analysis results
- Usage statistics or application telemetry
- System information or hardware details
- File names, paths, or directory structures
- Search queries or exploration activities

## 3. How We Use Information

### 3.1 License Management
- Verify license validity and prevent unauthorized use
- Provide license activation and support services
- Communicate important license or security updates

### 3.2 Customer Support
- Respond to support requests and technical questions
- Troubleshoot software issues and provide assistance
- Improve our software based on support feedback

### 3.3 Business Operations
- Process payments through third-party processors
- Maintain records for accounting and legal compliance
- Send important product announcements or updates

## 4. Third-Party Services

### 4.1 Payment Processing
- **Stripe**: Handles payment processing securely
- **We do not store**: Credit card numbers or payment details
- **Stripe's Privacy Policy**: Applies to payment data handling

### 4.2 AI Features (Optional)
- **OpenAI Integration**: You may optionally use AI features that require your own OpenAI API key
- **Your Control**: You provide your own API key and control all data transmission
- **No Tekmera Access**: We do not have access to your OpenAI interactions or data
- **OpenAI's Privacy Policy**: Applies to data sent to their services

### 4.3 Email Delivery
- **Automated Systems**: License delivery via email automation platforms
- **Minimal Data**: Only email address and license information transmitted
- **Secure Transmission**: All communications use encrypted channels

## 5. Data Storage and Security

### 5.1 Local Data Storage
- **License File**: Stored locally in `~/.tekmera/license.json` on your machine
- **No Cloud Storage**: We do not store your data in cloud services
- **Your Control**: You can delete local data at any time

### 5.2 Security Measures
- **Encryption**: All data transmission uses industry-standard encryption
- **Limited Access**: Only authorized personnel have access to customer data
- **Regular Reviews**: Security practices reviewed and updated regularly

### 5.3 Data Retention
- **License Records**: Maintained for legal and support purposes
- **Support Communications**: Retained for 2 years for service improvement
- **Payment Records**: Retained as required by applicable laws

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Access and Correction
- Request access to your personal data
- Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
- Contact support@tekmera.com for data requests

### 6.2 Data Deletion
- Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal requirements)
- Delete local license data by removing `~/.tekmera/license.json`
- Use `tekmera license deactivate` command to remove local license

### 6.3 Opt-Out Rights
- Unsubscribe from marketing emails using provided links
- Disable AI features by not providing OpenAI API keys
- Contact us to opt out of non-essential communications

## 7. International Data Transfers

### 7.1 Data Location

- All transfers comply with applicable privacy laws

### 7.2 Adequacy and Safeguards
